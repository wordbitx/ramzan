Events & Tips

Care tips from the hospital

Simple, honest guidance for everyday animal care. For anything serious, call us — don't wait on a tip.

Small Pet Tip

Rabbits & parrots need checks too

Small pets hide illness well. A rabbit that's off its food or a parrot that's unusually quiet needs attention the same day — message us online and we'll guide you.

Livestock Tip

Deworm the whole herd together

Deworming one animal while the rest of the herd stays untreated lets parasites cycle straight back. Plan herd-wide deworming and routine checkups together — we can schedule both in one farm visit.

Nutrition Tip

Match food to age & size

Puppies, adult dogs and senior cats all need different nutrition. When you order from our Pet Food Shop, ask on WhatsApp which option suits your animal's age, breed and health.
